How it worksكيف يعمل

  1. Install

    Download the installer and run it. No administrator password is needed. The app starts with a small demo database with made-up data, so you can try it at once.

  2. Connect your database

    In Settings > Connection, choose SQLite, MySQL, MariaDB or PostgreSQL, enter the details and click Test connection. Safest: a database login that can only read.

  3. Choose your AI

    In Settings > AI Provider, choose Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or a local model, and paste your own key. It is stored encrypted on your PC.

  4. Teach it your words

    Click Draft with AI… in the glossary: DataPilot suggests plain names for your tables and columns, and you correct them. Add your business definitions. With Enterprise, we draft this for you.

  5. Add your people

    Create an account for each person, give each a role, and choose which tables and columns each role may see.

  6. Ask

    Type a question. Read the table, ask for a chart, check the steps under Thought for…, and export to CSV or Excel.

  1. ثبّت التطبيق

    نزّل ملف التثبيت وشغّله. لا تحتاج إلى كلمة مرور المسؤول. يبدأ التطبيق بقاعدة بيانات تجريبية صغيرة ببيانات وهمية لتجرّبه فوراً.

  2. اربط قاعدة بياناتك

    في Settings > Connection اختر SQLite أو MySQL أو MariaDB أو PostgreSQL، وأدخل البيانات، وانقر Test connection. الأكثر أماناً: حساب قاعدة بيانات لا يملك إلا صلاحية القراءة.

  3. اختر الذكاء الاصطناعي

    في Settings > AI Provider اختر Claude أو OpenAI أو Gemini أو نموذجاً محلياً، والصق مفتاحك الخاص. يُحفظ المفتاح مشفّراً على جهازك.

  4. علّمه كلماتك

    انقر Draft with AI… في المسرد: يقترح DataPilot أسماء واضحة لجداولك وأعمدتك، وأنت تصحّحها. ثم أضف تعريفات العمل لديك. مع خطة Enterprise نكتب هذه المسودة لك.

  5. أضف فريقك

    أنشئ حساباً لكل شخص، وامنح كلاً منهم دوراً، واختر الجداول والأعمدة التي يراها كل دور.

  6. اسأل

    اكتب سؤالك. اقرأ الجدول، واطلب رسماً بيانياً، وراجع الخطوات تحت Thought for…، وصدّر النتيجة إلى CSV أو Excel.

DataPilot runs on your PC and talks to two things: your database and the AI you chose. We are not in the middle.

Stays on your PC and network

  • Your database and every row in it.

  • Your settings, glossary, definitions and audit log.

Goes to the AI you chose, with your key

  • Your question and the earlier questions of the same chat.
  • The names of the tables and columns it may need, with their glossary names, and your business definitions.

Goes to us, only if you choose

  • Nothing, unless your Super Admin connects the app to a DataPilot account.
  • Then: a setup file when you send it (names of tables and columns, never rows), licence and update checks, and a daily count of questions if you switch it on.
  • Never your questions, rows of data, passwords or users.

Read the install guideاقرأ دليل التثبيت

Ask questions about your company database in plain English or in Arabic, and get tables and charts back. DataPilot only reads your data. It can never change or delete it.

1. Install

  • Double-click the DataPilot AI setup file. Click Next, then Install. You do not need an administrator password.
  • If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. We will sign the installer in a later version.
  • Start it from the desktop shortcut or the Start menu: "DataPilot AI".

2. First start: create the Super Admin

  • The first time, the app asks you to create an account. You become the Super Admin of this installation.
  • Choose a long password and keep it safe.
  • The app then shows you a recovery code. Write it down and keep it in a safe place. It is shown only once. If you forget your password, click Forgot password? on the sign-in window, then enter your user name, the recovery code and a new password. The code works once.
  • To make a new code later (the old one stops working), open Profile and click Create a recovery code....
  • To leave the app, click Sign out at the bottom of the left bar.

3. Connect your database

  • Until you connect your own database, the app starts on a small demo database with made-up data, so you can try it. Connecting your own database, below, replaces it.
  • Safest: ask the person who manages your database server for a read-only login. Here is an example for MariaDB or MySQL: 
    CREATE USER 'datapilot_reader'@'%' IDENTIFIED BY 'a-strong-password';
GRANT SELECT ON your_database.* TO 'datapilot_reader'@'%';
  • In the app, open Settings > Connection. Choose your database type and fill in the host, the port, the database name, the user and the password. Click Test connection, then Save.
  • The first question after connecting reads the list of your tables. With hundreds of tables this takes a few seconds, once.
  • Every query has a time limit, so your server is never held up.

4. Choose the AI and add its key

  • Open Settings > AI Provider and choose a provider: Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or another one on the list.
  • Get a key from your provider's website, paste it into the key box, click Test provider, then Save. Gemini has a free plan, with limits per minute and per day. If the app says the AI is busy, wait a minute.
  • The key is stored encrypted on this computer.

5. Draft the glossary

Your tables and columns may have short or technical names. The glossary gives them plain names, so questions and answers make sense.

  • Open Settings > Glossary and click Draft with AI.... Read the draft, fix any name that is wrong, and save.
  • Settings > Business definitions holds your own rules for the words your company uses (what counts, and what does not). Answer the setup questions once and every answer follows your rules. Under each answer, "Using your definition of..." shows which rules were used.

6. Add your colleagues

  • Open Settings > Users and click Add user. Give each person a role. Give people only the access they need.
  • Settings > Data access lets you hide sensitive tables from some roles.

7. Ask questions

  • Open Chat and type a question in English or in Arabic. Example: "How many records were added last month?"
  • Answers come as a table, and you can ask for a chart.
  • Click Thought for... under an answer to see how the app got it.

8. Where things are kept

  • Accounts, passwords and keys: %APPDATA%\DataPilotAI on this computer (passwords and keys are encrypted).
  • Settings: config.yaml in the program folder, %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\DataPilot AI.
  • Logs: the file datapilot.log in the logs folder inside the program folder. To open that folder, click About in the left bar, then Open log folder. Send this file to us when something goes wrong.
  • Exports (Excel, CSV and the setup file): the save window starts in Documents\DataPilot, or in the folder you used last. When a file is saved, a box offers Open and Show in folder.
  • What goes to the AI: your question, the names of your tables and columns, and a few example values from columns that have only a handful of different values (like a status). Your data rows are not sent to it.
  • To remove the app: Windows Settings > Apps > DataPilot AI > Uninstall. To also remove accounts, delete %APPDATA%\DataPilotAI.
  • This guide is also in the Start menu: "DataPilot AI - Read me first".

9. Licence

Without a licence the app runs on the Free plan (Chat, tables, basic charts). If you received a licence file: Settings > Features & licence > Install licence...

No. The AI may write only a read-only query, and DataPilot checks every query before it runs and refuses anything else. We also advise a database login that can only read, so your database server enforces it too.

Your question, the earlier questions of the same chat, the names of the tables and columns it may need with their glossary names, and your business definitions.

A few sample values from text columns are sent only while the Send sample values switch is on (Settings > AI Provider; it is on in a new installation, and your Super Admin can switch it off). If a query fails, the query and the database's error message are sent so the AI can fix it.

Whole rows, passwords and the app's user names are never sent.

Do you see my data?

No. The app runs on your PC and talks to your database and to the AI provider you chose. We receive something only if your Super Admin connects the app to a DataPilot account, for example to send us a setup file. Where are passwords and keys kept?

On the PC, encrypted, in %APPDATA%\DataPilotAI.

Does this website track me?

No. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or trackers. It remembers your language and theme in your browser's storage, on your device only.
